// THE GARAGE

Everything a typing game should be

No ads, no pay-to-win, no cloud accounts. Just racing, rewards, and real progress you can measure.

Race adaptive AI

Up to 9 opponents with real personalities — nervous, aggressive, champion. No cheating: cars move exactly as fast as you type.

Earn coins, build a garage

Win races to earn coins, then spend them on 21 cars, paints, wheel rims, and exhaust trails. Purely cosmetic — skill decides every race.

300+ built-in passages

Animals, space, science, history, quotes, and more — across three difficulty levels. Guided lessons and practice drills included.

Analytics that matter

WPM, accuracy, most-missed keys, a typing heatmap, and daily/weekly trends — so students (and teachers) see improvement.

Teacher mode

Password-protected. Create student accounts, reset progress, unlock content, manage text packs, and export reports.

Achievements & certificates

12 achievements, practice streaks, and printable PDF certificates with a unique ID as students complete each level.

100% offline

Runs on the most locked-down school PC. No internet, no accounts, no data leaving the room — everything lives locally.

One-click backup

Export or import a single backup file containing everything — accounts, garage, analytics, certificates, leaderboards.

// FOR TEACHERS

Made for the classroom

CarTyper was designed with schools in mind: it runs entirely offline, needs no student accounts online, and gives teachers full control.

Get CarTyper
  • Guest mode & local accounts — students just sit down and race; no sign-ups, no emails.
  • Teacher mode — create and manage student accounts, reset progress, unlock content, view the leaderboard, and export reports.
  • Printable certificates — students earn PDF certificates with unique IDs for completing each typing level.
  • No data leaves the school — everything is stored on the device. Back it up with one click and restore anywhere.
